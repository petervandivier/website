It can be tempting think about the database as a static asset. We plan capacity considering size-of-data on-disk; we check for physical & logical corruption; we create & test backup media. These are priority zero responsibilities which all consider the at-rest (mostly) data; but data at-rest is data unused.

> [**liminal**](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/liminal) <sub>(adjective)</sub>
> 
> `lim·​i·​nal`
> 
> 2: _of, relating to, or being an intermediate state, phase, or condition_: <kbd>IN-BETWEEN</kbd>, <kbd>TRANSITIONAL</kbd>

The movement of data to and from storage and consumer is what generates value. As data specialists, we must prepare to consider all aspects of the data lifecycle - from provisioning of the storage engine to consumption by and submission from downstream applications.

> "_In God we trust. All others must bring data._"

As a part of whole-lifecycle awareness, we specialize on simplification and exposure of previously opaque components of the stack. Human-understandable representation of architecture is my personal favorite part of infrastructure-as-code solutions. Minification and refinement of this data into self-describing structures is - in my humble opinion - a worthy cause.
